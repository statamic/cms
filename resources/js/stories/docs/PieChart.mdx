# PieChart
A pie chart for up to four segments. Legend items may be clickable, and `segments` can preserve the original pie while displaying a focused segment breakdown. When every item has an `image` (and there are at most two), the slices are filled with those images instead of colors.
<Canvas of={PieChartStories._DocsIntro} sourceState={'shown'} />

Keep the items ordered by significance and supply an `accessibleLabel` that states the same result in plain language. Pie charts reject more than four segments; group smaller values before passing them in.

## Interactive breakdown
Set `clickable: true` on a legend item and handle `select` to reveal its detail. On the detail chart, pass the original items as `segments` and the selected zero-based index as `focusedIndex`.
<Canvas of={PieChartStories._InteractiveBreakdown} sourceState={'shown'} />

## Focused segment
This is the detail state by itself. The original segments retain the shape of the pie while the new `items` populate its legend.
<Canvas of={PieChartStories._FocusedSegment} sourceState={'shown'} />

## Custom markers
Replace legend swatches through the `marker` slot. It receives the current `item` and `index`.
<Canvas of={PieChartStories._CustomMarkers} sourceState={'shown'} />

## Image slices
When every item carries an `image` and there are no more than two, each slice is filled with its image. Items may also carry a `badge` shown in the legend.
